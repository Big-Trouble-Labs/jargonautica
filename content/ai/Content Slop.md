# Content Slop

## Plain View

Low-quality generated material produced at volume because it is cheap: filler articles, fake reviews, spam books, AI-generated video churned out for advertising revenue. It degrades the usefulness of search, marketplaces and social platforms, and it is an externality nobody is obliged to clean up.

## Technical View

Economic drivers are near-zero marginal production cost against platform incentives that reward volume. Effects include displacement of human-authored material in ranking, contamination of future training corpora leading to quality degradation across generations, and erosion of trust in unlabelled content. Countermeasures include provenance standards, ranking changes penalising low-effort content, and platform policies requiring disclosure, all currently outpaced by production volume.
