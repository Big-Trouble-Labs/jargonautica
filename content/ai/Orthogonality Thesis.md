# Orthogonality Thesis

## Plain View

The claim that how capable a system is and what it wants are independent. Being extremely good at achieving goals does not make a system's goals wise or humane. It rules out the comfortable assumption that a sufficiently intelligent system would naturally arrive at good values.

## Technical View

Bostrom's thesis that any level of intelligence is compatible in principle with any final goal. The counterargument is that goals learned from human-generated data are not drawn from an arbitrary space, and that current systems acquire values and capabilities from the same training process rather than independently. The thesis is a claim about possibility, not about what training procedures actually produce.
