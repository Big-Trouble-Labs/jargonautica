# Dual Use

## Plain View

Capability that helps and harms with equal facility. The same chemistry knowledge that supports research supports misuse; the same code ability that finds vulnerabilities to fix finds them to exploit. It means capability restriction is a blunt instrument, and much of the difficulty in AI safety policy comes from this.

## Technical View

Concentrated in cyber, chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear domains, and increasingly in autonomous capability. Policy responses include capability evaluation before release, tiered access with know-your-customer requirements, refusal training for specific request classes, and staged release. The counter-argument is that restriction imposes real costs on legitimate research while determined actors have alternatives, which is why marginal uplift rather than absolute capability is the measure used.
