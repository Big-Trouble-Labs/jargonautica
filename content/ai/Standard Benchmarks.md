# Standard Benchmarks

## Plain View

The named tests everyone quotes. Each measures something quite specific, and the names give no clue what. Knowing roughly what the common ones cover is the difference between reading a model announcement critically and taking the bar chart at face value.

## Technical View

Broad knowledge and reasoning suites test multiple-choice academic questions across subjects; mathematical benchmarks range from grade-school word problems to competition level; coding benchmarks measure functional correctness of generated programs against test suites, with agentic variants requiring changes to real repositories; long-context suites test retrieval and aggregation across large inputs. Most saturate within a couple of years of release, which is why leaderboards churn and why comparisons across time are unreliable.
