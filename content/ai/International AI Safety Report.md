# International AI Safety Report

## Plain View

A periodic synthesis of the evidence on AI capabilities and risks, produced by a large international group of researchers and intended to give policymakers a shared factual baseline in the way climate assessments do. It is the closest thing available to an agreed statement of what is and is not known.

## Technical View

Commissioned following the international summit process and chaired by Yoshua Bengio with a panel of expert contributors nominated by participating states. It surveys general-purpose AI capabilities, risks including malicious use, malfunction and systemic effects, and technical mitigation approaches, distinguishing established findings from contested ones. Its value for practitioners is as a citable, non-partisan reference; check the current edition, as it is updated.
